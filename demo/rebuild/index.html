FAQ

Where and when is the conference?

Hilton Union Square, 333 O'Farrell St, San Francisco. Workshops on Tue, Nov 10; talks and expo on Wed–Thu, Nov 11–12, 2026.

How do I get a ticket?

Apply with your email. Applying is free and doesn't reserve a seat. If you're approved, you get an email with a link to buy a ticket ($1,499 + fees; workshop add-on $299 + fees).

What's the format?

A day of AIE workshops, two days of single-track sessions, the Summit Expo, and breaks for the hallway track.

Who should attend?

Engineers building coding agents, founders and engineering leaders rolling out coding and internal agents, and researchers advancing AI software engineering.

Are talks recorded?

Yes. Both days were livestreamed and are on YouTube, with a reading edition and transcript for each session.
