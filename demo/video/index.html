Rehearsal

Test your site's visitor journeys, before visitors do.
Rehearse real visitor journeys in a browser. Get cited findings and a rebuild spec.
The problem

Sites get judged by opinion,
not by trying what visitors try.

"Looks fine to me"Taste decides what ships.
No journey evidenceNobody checks if a visitor can reach the schedule, pricing or signup.
Found after launchReal users find the dead ends first.
How it works

Give it a goal. Get back checked journeys, findings and a spec.

01 · INTENTInformation · Quotation · ProposalSort the visitor goal into RFI, RFQ or RFP.
02 · ENDPOINTSWeighted endpointsFind the pages visitors want (pricing, docs, signup, demo) and weight them by intent.
03 · JOURNEYSJourney decision treeMap unique page states. Jev scores each action. Run 300 seeded journeys.
04 · VERIFYBrowser verificationReplay the riskiest paths in a real browser, desktop and mobile, with screenshots.
05 · ANALYZEKrug-cited findingsverified-failure · reproducible-concern · hypothesis. Each one cites a Krug principle.
06 · SPECspec.md rebuild planChanges ranked by impact, each tied to evidence, plus a rebuild plan.
