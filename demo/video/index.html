Rehearsal

UX verification for websites, grounded in Krug.
Rehearse real visitor journeys in a browser. Get cited findings and a rebuild spec.
The problem

Websites get reviewed by opinion,
not by rehearsing real visitor journeys.

"Looks fine to me"Stakeholder taste decides what ships.
No journey evidenceNobody checks whether a visitor can actually reach the schedule, pricing or signup.
Found after launchReal users hit the dead ends first.
How it works

Goal in. Verified journeys, cited findings and a spec out.

01 · INTENTInformation · Quotation · ProposalClassify the visitor goal as RFI / RFQ / RFP.
02 · ENDPOINTSWeighted endpointsDetect answer, docs, pricing, register, demo-booking… and weight them by intent.
03 · JOURNEYSJourney decision treeDiscover deduped page states; Jev scores every action; 1,000 seeded journeys.
04 · VERIFYBrowser verificationRiskiest paths replayed in a real browser, desktop + mobile, with assertions and screenshots.
05 · ANALYZEKrug-cited findingsverified-failure · reproducible-concern · hypothesis. Every finding cites a Krug principle.
06 · SPECspec.md rebuild planChanges ranked by impact, each traced to evidence, plus a phased rebuild plan.
